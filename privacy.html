Privacy Policy

ViewMate PDF Reader

Effective date: November 9, 2026 · Last updated: November 9, 2026

OligoLabs ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how ViewMate PDF Reader ("ViewMate", "the App") handles information when you use our Android application.

Our goal is to provide a simple, reliable, and privacy-friendly PDF reading experience. ViewMate is designed to work locally on your device without requiring an account, cloud storage, or an internet connection.

By using ViewMate, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

In short

  • No internet permission. ViewMate's manifest declares no INTERNET permission, so Android does not let the App make network connections. An automated test guards this.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. Data collected by us: none.
  • Your PDFs stay on your device. They are never uploaded, and the originals are never modified.
  • Everything the App remembers is local and is not part of cloud backups.

1. Information We Collect

ViewMate does not collect, transmit, or share your personal information with OligoLabs or external servers.

The App does not require:

  • Account registration or login.
  • Your name or email address.
  • Your phone number.
  • Your location.
  • Personal profile information.

We do not use advertising services, analytics tools, crash-reporting services, or tracking technologies in the current version of ViewMate.

2. PDF Files and Document Access

ViewMate allows you to open, read, search, mark up, fill, sign, organize, and manage PDF documents stored on your device.

Documents are accessed through Android's file picker, folders you choose to grant, or "Open with" from another app, always with your permission.

Your PDF documents:

  • Are processed locally on your device.
  • Are not uploaded to OligoLabs servers.
  • Are not stored in a ViewMate cloud account.
  • Are not transmitted to us for analysis or advertising.
  • Are never modified in place. Annotations, filled forms, signatures, merges, splits, and page edits are always saved as a new PDF copy through Android's "Save as" screen, in a location you choose.

ViewMate may use locally generated document identifiers to recognize previously opened PDFs, including when a document is moved or renamed. These identifiers are calculated and stored locally and are not transmitted to external servers.

You remain responsible for the PDF files stored on your device.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide its features, ViewMate stores certain application information locally.

This may include:

  • Reading history: Information about recently opened PDF documents.
  • Reading progress: The last page you viewed so you can continue reading where you stopped.
  • Bookmarks: Pages you have marked for quick access, including any notes you attach to them.
  • Favorites: PDF documents you have marked as favorites.
  • Collections: Your locally created PDF groups and organization preferences.
  • Annotations and notes: Personal notes, highlights, underlines, drawings, pins, sticky notes, text boxes, and other editing information created within the App.
  • Saved signature: If you draw a signature and choose to reuse it, the drawing (an image of your handwriting) is kept on your device so you can place it again.
  • Search index: To search across all your PDFs, ViewMate can build a private index of the text in your documents. The index lives only on your device. You can turn it off and clear it at any time in Settings. Password-protected PDFs are never indexed.
  • Application preferences: Your selected theme (light, dark, or system), reading layout, page fit, and other settings.

This information is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers. It is excluded from Android cloud backups, so it is not copied to your Google account or any other cloud service by the App.

4. Permissions

ViewMate uses Android permissions and document access mechanisms only when necessary to provide its functionality.

Depending on how you use the App, this may include:

  • Document access: Allows you to select and open PDF files through Android's picker or "Open with".
  • Folder access: Allows you to grant access to folders containing PDF documents so ViewMate can browse available files.
  • Optional "Scan this device" (all-files access): Everyday use does not need broad storage access. All-files access is requested only if you tap "Scan this device" to find every PDF on your phone, and the App explains the request first. You can decline it and grant individual folders instead. This option is not available on Android 10.
  • File sharing: Allows you to intentionally share PDF documents through Android's sharing interface.
  • No network permission: ViewMate does not declare the INTERNET permission.

ViewMate does not use these permissions to collect personal information or upload your documents to external servers.

You can manage or revoke applicable permissions through your Android device settings.

5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

OligoLabs does not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties.

The current version of ViewMate does not integrate:

  • Advertising networks.
  • Third-party analytics services.
  • User tracking services.
  • Cloud synchronization providers.
  • Remote document storage services.

Read aloud. ViewMate's read-aloud feature uses only the offline text-to-speech voices installed on your device. The text of your document is not sent anywhere.

However, when you intentionally share a PDF using Android's sharing interface, the selected application receives the document according to your instructions.

Any third-party application you choose to use is governed by its own privacy policy and terms.

6. External Links

PDF documents may contain hyperlinks.

When you open a hyperlink, ViewMate redirects you to your device's external browser or another application, because ViewMate cannot go online itself.

We do not control external websites or applications and are not responsible for their privacy practices, content, or security.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of external services you visit.

7. Data Security

We take a privacy-focused approach to application design.

ViewMate processes PDF documents and stores its application information locally on your device. Because the App has no network permission, it cannot send your documents or reading information to us or anyone else.

We do not operate remote servers that receive your PDF documents or locally stored reading information.

The security of information stored on your device also depends on your Android operating system, device security settings, and physical access to your device.

We recommend using a device lock and keeping your operating system updated.

A signature drawn in ViewMate is a picture of your handwriting placed on the page. It is not a certificate-based digital signature.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

ViewMate does not maintain personal information on OligoLabs servers.

Locally stored application information remains on your device until you delete it or remove the application data.

You can manage your information through the following methods:

  • Clear reading history: Use the Clear history option within ViewMate. This removes the corresponding reading history records but does not delete the original PDF files.
  • Turn off and clear the search index: Use the search index option in Settings.
  • Remove bookmarks, favorites, collections, notes, and your saved signature: Use the relevant features within the application to remove locally stored items where supported.
  • Clear application data: Use Android's application settings to clear ViewMate's locally stored application data.
  • Uninstall ViewMate: Uninstalling the application removes its locally stored application data, subject to Android operating system behavior and any files you independently saved elsewhere, such as PDF copies you saved.

Deleting application data does not automatically delete your original PDF documents, or the new copies you saved, from their storage locations.

Because ViewMate does not maintain user accounts or store user information on our servers, there is no account deletion process or remote personal-data deletion request required.

9. Children's Privacy

ViewMate does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

The App does not require account creation or submission of personal information.

If you believe that personal information has been inadvertently provided to us, please contact us using the email address provided below.

10. Internet Connectivity

ViewMate is designed as an offline-first PDF reader.

The current version does not require an internet connection, and does not request internet permission, to open and read locally stored PDF documents or use its core features.

However, opening external hyperlinks or sharing documents through third-party applications may involve internet connectivity or external services outside ViewMate's control.

The ViewMate website, where you can find the download link, is separate from the App. It does not use advertising or analytics trackers.
