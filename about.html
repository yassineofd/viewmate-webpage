About us

A PDF reader that minds its own business.

ViewMate is an Android app for reading, marking up, filling, signing and organizing PDFs. It is made by OligoLabs, and it is built around one idea: your documents belong on your device and nowhere else.

What we build

ViewMate PDF Reader is a simple, fast and dependable way to work with the PDFs on your phone. You can read and search them, highlight and add notes, fill forms, sign, merge, split and organize pages, and pick up exactly where you left off.

It works fully offline. There is no account to create, no cloud to sync with, and nothing to configure before you open your first document.

What we believe

Private by design

ViewMate declares no internet permission, so Android does not let it make network connections. We do not collect data because the app has no way to send it.

Your originals stay safe

Annotations, filled forms, signatures and page edits are always saved as a new copy. The original file is never modified in place.

Simple and quick

No ads, no sign-up and no clutter. Open a PDF and get on with what you were doing.

Who is behind it

OligoLabs is an independent developer. ViewMate is our PDF reader for Android, published on Google Play under the package name com.oligolabs.viewmate. It runs on Android 7.0 (API 24) and newer.

We read the feedback we receive, and it shapes what gets built next. If something is missing or does not work the way you expect, we would like to hear about it.
